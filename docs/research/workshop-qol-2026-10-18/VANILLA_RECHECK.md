# Vanilla comparison of the complete prior QoL list

Every one of the prior 115 entries, plus the previously removed inventory-search entry, has been compared. Steam build **25485521**; full engine semantic version UNKNOWN. No game/server launch or third-party execution. Exact reviewed vanilla files, hashes, lexical scopes and selected anchor lines are in [vanilla-review.json](vanilla-review.json).

The review removes **37 native-baseline proposals**, holds **33 unproven proposals** outside the ranking, merges **2 duplicates**, and retains **44 entries**: **20 current Essentials entries** and **24 scoped additions**. The existing container-inspection foundation is preserved under nested-view rather than counted twice. All 21 original Essentials IMPLEMENTED/PARTIAL/FOUNDATION identifiers remain represented.

REMOVE_VANILLA means the proposed outcome is supplied by native controls or has no specified addition beyond them. RETAIN_DELTA identifies a narrower input/display/planning difference in the inspected native Lua paths; it does not claim exhaustive engine-wide absence. HOLD_UNPROVEN is excluded from the ranking because renderer, engine, usability or authority coverage remains uncertain. KEEP_EXISTING follows the user instruction to retain Essentials source/foundations; it is not proof that every part is novel.

UI information and UI workflow are compared separately. For example, vanilla already displays condition and capacity; the retained proposals concern hotbar-slot condition or simultaneous container-button fill cues. They add no new facts or gameplay state. Generic controller support, translation, recipes/requirements, favorites, map legend/centering/sharing and ordinary medical-consent prompts are baseline.
