## Five new proposals selected

- **Choose an exact whole-item transfer quantity** — Add an explicit count for a finite native transfer of whole items. Do not duplicate native one/half/all choices or Quartermaster shopping targets.
- **Item and recipe action shortcuts** — Own the workflow that invokes a known native item/recipe action. Generic UI controls may be optional helpers; exclude a general hotbar replacement, theme/layout engine and PC app host.
- **Queue selected vanilla vehicle-part services** — Coordinate a finite player-selected native mechanics itinerary only. No general vehicle simulation, CAT qualification system, clothing-maintenance system or changed service requirements.
- **Queue selected eligible native books** — Coordinate a finite native-book reading list. Exclude custom study, research, progression, journal recovery and recorded-media playback/device scheduling. Knowledge & Wisdom retains those domain contracts.
- **Mark an owned book or media item for later** — Local, explicitly chosen seen-for-later mark on an accessible item. Do not grant read state, reveal hidden titles, create a research/study ledger, add shopping demands or duplicate Dibs lists.
