# QoL ownership and portfolio coverage audit

The 44-entry QoL shortlist contains work already covered by other projects. The clearest duplicate is **selected-area cleanup in Auto Clean**. **Quartermaster already owns designated storage and logistics**; Essentials should retain its smaller seeded-sort workflow without rebuilding those rules. General HUD/inventory styling was incorrectly assigned to SPXTRM: **UI owns shared presentation; SPXTRM owns lighting and optics**. Medical treatment belongs to **Health Care**, not the blocked Medical Checks scaffold.

## Scope and evidence

[VERIFIED — local source/documents] Reviewed 28 primary local project folders, excluding alternate Medical Checks/worktree checkouts as separate products. CATConstruction is an editable third-party integration copy, not assumed team-owned code. Read each current README and project instructions, inventoried version/branch/HEAD and metadata candidates, and corroborated the central overlaps in targeted source paths. The machine-readable [ledger](portfolio-ownership.json) records portable evidence hashes, line anchors, branches, dirty state and metadata candidates.

[INFERRED] Ownership below is a recommendation from those declared scopes and reviewed source, not authorization to move code, impose dependencies, migrate saves or broaden a project. NOT_FOUND_IN_REVIEWED_SCOPE is deliberately weaker than proof of absence. Related APIs/foundations are not completed player workflows. Essentials coverage labels remain unchanged.

Only Essentials remote freshness was checked: source `ff59b1e877e9cc09e1481e33bb04e72356eafb81`, matching upstream, zero behind main, clean before this documentation edit. Other projects are exact local snapshots, not claims about their latest remote, main branch, Workshop package or enabled mod set. Buildable Fences, Dog Training, Eyewear, Power and Quartermaster had preserved local changes; none was edited. Six folders had no Git identity; four of those also lacked root VERSION. No identity/version is invented.

## Recommended boundaries

- Essentials: compact vanilla workflows, exact references, bounded read models and current source features. Retain standalone behavior; adapter design is separate work.
- Quartermaster: destination policy, sorting, shopping/Dibs, supply services, carry logistics and ledger accounting. Rooms: geometry/designations and its explicit permission boundary. Auto Clean: cleaning and any future corpse-cleanup workflow.
- UI: reusable controls and planned broad presentation adapters. Domain mods retain facts, visibility, gameplay callbacks and authority. PC Framework: powered world-computer application host only.
- Power: energy, devices and generator data/calculations. SPXTRM: lighting, bulbs, emission/exposure and optical presentation.
- Health Care: examination and treatment. Medical Checks: consent-policy research/settings while enforcement is blocked. Knowledge & Wisdom: registered knowledge, research and persistent study; Essentials may retain local native-media convenience.
- Harvest: crop work. Waterworks: water resources. Provisions: food processes. Junk Drawer: salvage. Earthworks/CAT: their terrain and machine state.
